# BigFatFile Audit Log

BigFatFile provides out-of-the-box support for AuditLogging. This is a time-based log (a "blame" track) of all operations performed on a Drive (or Account).

## Free Tier

The AuditEntry is available by defualt in all plans, including Free. What differs is the amount of time
the log entries are kept in the log:

- Free plan: 3 days
- Pro plan: 30-90 days (? depends of intl standard requirements)
- Enterprise plan: 90-365 days (?)
